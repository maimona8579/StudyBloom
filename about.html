🌸 About StudyBloom

StudyBloom is a cozy, aesthetic study-planning companion created to bring warmth, calmness, and organization to your daily student life. Inspired by gentle desk setups and sticky-note study boards, it helps you focus on steady, sustainable growth.

🎯 Our Goals

We believe learning should be encouraging, not overwhelming. StudyBloom combines time management with mindful wellness—offering gentle daily schedules, bite-sized goals, hydration tracking, and evidence-backed study techniques.

🔒 Private by Design

Every student gets a completely private space. StudyBloom has no accounts, no trackers, and no remote servers. Everything is stored purely inside this browser on your device.

💡 Safe Usage & Tips

Because your planner lives in your browser's local storage, clearing your browser cache or site data will erase your saved items. Please remember not to store passwords or sensitive personal information.
